# AnyToneCPS — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 5, 2026

AnyToneCPS is a hobby project built and maintained by one person in their spare time. This page
explains what data the app touches, in plain language.

## Summary

AnyToneCPS does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to the developer or to any
third party. It is a codeplug programming tool for the AnyTone D890UV DMR radio: it reads and
writes your radio's configuration (channels, zones, contacts, and other settings) directly between
your device and the radio itself, over a USB cable. There is no account, no sign-in, no backend run
by the developer, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Data stored on your device

- **Project files** — the codeplug you create or edit (channels, zones, contacts, and radio
  settings) is saved as a project file in storage you choose on your device. This file never
  leaves your device unless you choose to share, back up, or transfer it yourself.
- **App preferences** — basic settings such as your last-used project location, kept locally.

Nothing above is ever transmitted to the developer.

## USB access to your radio

AnyToneCPS requests permission to access a connected AnyTone D890UV radio over USB when you plug
one in and choose to read from or write to it. This is a direct, local connection between your
device and the radio — no data from this exchange is sent anywhere else. On devices without USB
Host support, the app still installs and works for editing project files; only the radio
connection is unavailable.

## Internet permission

The app currently requests Android's INTERNET permission, reserved for a planned future feature
(looking up nearby repeater information from an online database) that is not yet implemented. No
version of the app released so far makes any network request. This policy will be updated, with a
new effective date, before that feature ships, to describe exactly what it sends and to where.

## Third parties

AnyToneCPS includes no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not share data
with any third party.

## Children's privacy

AnyToneCPS does not knowingly collect data from anyone, regardless of age, for the reasons
described above.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be reflected here with a new effective date.

## Contact

This is a solo project with no dedicated support channel. Questions or concerns can be raised via
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/EtherGhost/AnyToneCPS/issues), or by email at

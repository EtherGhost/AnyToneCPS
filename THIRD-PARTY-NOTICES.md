## anytone-cps (xbenkozx)

<https://github.com/xbenkozx/anytone-cps>

An early version of AnyToneCPS used this project as a reference for the
D890UV codeplug byte layout and some settings logic (e.g. alarm settings and
APRS). Many of its layouts turned out to be wrong for the D890UV, and they
have since been re-verified against real hardware and the vendor CPS. Portions
derived from it remain, and are used under its MIT License:
